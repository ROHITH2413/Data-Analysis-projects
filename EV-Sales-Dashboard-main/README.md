Electric Vehicle Market Analysis – India 🚗⚡  

📌 Project Overview  
This project provides an in-depth analysis of the Indian Electric Vehicle (EV) market to support AtliQ Motors in launching their EV/Hybrid vehicles in India. The analysis covers sales trends, penetration rates, top manufacturers, government incentives, and market opportunities to identify the best strategies for entering the EV sector.  

📊 Key Insights  
- EV Market Growth:  
  - 2-Wheeler revenue grew 276% (2022–2024).  
  - 4-Wheeler revenue grew 364% (2022–2024).  

- Top EV Makers (FY 2024):  
  - 2-Wheeler: OLA Electric, TVS, Ather  
  - 4-Wheeler: TATA Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, MG Motors  

- Bottom EV Makers (FY 2024):  
  - 2-Wheeler: Battre Electric, Revolt, Kinetic Green  
  - 4-Wheeler: Mercedes-Benz, KIA Motors, Volvo Auto India  

- Top States (Penetration & Sales): Karnataka, Delhi, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Maharashtra  

- Government Incentives:  
  - Central FAME-II Scheme (₹15,000/kWh for 2-wheelers, ₹10,000/kWh for 4-wheelers).  
  - State subsidies strongest in Maharashtra, Haryana, and Delhi.  

- Consumer Drivers:  
  - Lower operating costs  
  - Environmental concerns  
  - Government subsidies  
  - Innovation (auto-driving, auto-parking, advanced safety features)  

- Best States for Manufacturing Plants: Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Maharashtra

🎯 Recommendations for AtliQ Motors  
1. Innovation-led Strategy – focus on extended range, faster charging, and smart features.  
2. Strategic Manufacturing – Tamil Nadu or Gujarat for subsidies and existing EV ecosystem.  
3. Marketing Approach – Position EVs as cost-effective, eco-friendly, and technologically advanced.
